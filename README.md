# Field Manual

A dependency-free, bilingual (EN-US / PT-BR) library of interactive board-game rulebooks. Run `python server.py` and visit `http://127.0.0.1:8765`. Sniper Elite's four original source manuals, the authorized Avalon: The Riven Veil Retail Edition manual, the authorized Pagan: The Fate of Roanoke manual, the authorized Odin's Ravens manual, the authorized Cangaceiros manual, the authorized Coup booklet, the authorized Munchkin core rulesheet, and the authorized Pandemic rulebooks (core plus the On the Brink, In the Lab and State of Emergency expansions) are in `manuals/`; guides link to these same PDFs locally and on GitHub Pages, with anchors to cited pages. You can also open `index.html` directly.

The main game index includes a QR code that opens `https://rbasniak.github.io/boardgames`, so players at the table can open the published guide on their own devices. If the published address changes, update `TABLE_SHARE_URL` in `app.js` and regenerate `assets/share-qr.svg`. `app.js` is only the index and the hash router. Each game lives in its own script: `sniper-elite.js`, `not-enough-mana.js`, `avalon-the-riven-veil.js`, `pagan.js`, `odins-ravens.js`, `western-legends.js`, `lotr-fate-of-the-fellowship.js`, `witcher-path-of-destiny.js`, `cangaceiros.js`, `lotr-duel-for-middle-earth.js`, `coup.js`, `munchkin.js`, and `pandemic.js`.

The Sniper Elite guide covers multiplayer rules only. Its map, expansion, Sniper, and optional-rule selections filter the relevant setup steps, components, map rules, turns, examples, and glossary. Optional Special Ops lets players select a rank and allocate exactly that many edges per side; repeated edge types are supported. Match settings and setup checklist progress are saved in browser `localStorage`. Changing the map, expansions, Sniper choice, rank, or optional rules clears checklist progress; **Uncheck all** clears only the checklist and keeps the match configuration, language, and team.

To add another game, use the repository skill at `.github/skills/interactive-rulebook/SKILL.md`. In Copilot CLI, load new skills with `/skills reload`, then ask to create the next game using `/interactive-rulebook`. The skill covers source auditing, repository-local manuals, match options, complete bilingual rules, original iconography, setup checklists, and verification.

Not Enough Mana uses that skill. Its guide is at `#/not-enough-mana` and stores its match separately from Sniper Elite. The Maleficus Praecepta booklet is not in this repository, because redistribution rights were not confirmed. Page links still use `manuals/not-enough-mana_core.pdf#page=N` locally and on GitHub Pages. Individual spell, curse, artifact, and event texts stay on the cards; the booklet does not list them.

Avalon: The Riven Veil uses `#/avalon-the-riven-veil` and stores its 2–4 player match separately. The guide covers the Retail core rules, all four factions, and the four configurable rule modes. Its authorized source manual is `manuals/avalon_core.pdf`, aligned with the route in `server.py` and the references in `avalon-the-riven-veil.js`; the cover is under `assets/covers/`, and individual icon crops from manual pages 24–25 are under `assets/avalon/icons/`. Faction-board and individual card text takes precedence over the summarized rules.

Pagan: The Fate of Roanoke also uses that skill. Its guide is at `#/pagan` and stores its match separately from the other games. The rulebook PDF is included in this repository at `manuals/pagan-fate-of-roanoke_core.pdf`, with redistribution authorized. Build your match offers one real choice: the printed "Welcome to Roanoke" scenario with its fixed 60-card starter decks, or the standard game with custom-built 50-card decks. The Caretakers are always the Green villager faction in this core box (The Stout is always red/Witch, Voluntary Recluses is always blue/Hunter); the "Beyond the Palisades" expansion and its content packs are not covered. Individual card texts beyond the starter decks and the 9 villager skills stay on the physical cards.

Odin's Ravens also uses that skill. Its guide is at `#/odins-ravens` and stores its match separately from the other games. The rulebook PDF is included in this repository at `manuals/odins-ravens_core.pdf`, with redistribution authorized. This core box has no expansions, maps, or optional rules, so Build your match has nothing to configure; the guide opens straight into the full two-page booklet: Setup, Goal & Flight, Trickery (all 8 Loki card actions, cropped individually from the manual), and Ending the turn/game.

Western Legends also uses that skill. Its guide is at `#/western-legends` and stores its match (`field-manual.western-legends.v1`) and checklist (`field-manual.western-legends.setup.v1`) separately. It covers the core game, Ante Up, Blood Money with its five game modes, Fistful of Extras, The Good, the Bad and the Handsome, the Wild Bunch of Extras rules, and the official FAQ, whose rulings are folded into the text with page links. Build your match starts with the game mode, then players, expansions, story system, Legendary Point target, and optional systems. Choices a mode or expansion makes required or impossible are fixed or hidden, and any automatic adjustment is announced. The manuals are under `manuals/western-legends_*.pdf` and the code is `western-legends.js`. Three source files were edited from the supplied combined PDF. `western-legends_core.pdf` holds the 24 core rulebook pages. `western-legends_blood-money.pdf` has the 12-page Blood Money Game Modes book appended after the expansion rulebook. `western-legends_wild-bunch-of-extras.pdf` keeps only its printed pp. 45–48. The page links in `BOOKS` translate printed page numbers into these files' PDF pages, including the two-pages-per-sheet spreads in Ante Up and Blood Money. Card texts, character boards and individual story cards stay on the components.

The Lord of the Rings: Fate of the Fellowship also uses that skill. Its guide is at `#/lotr-fate-of-the-fellowship` and stores its match (`field-manual.lotr-fate-of-the-fellowship.v1`) and checklist (`field-manual.lotr-fate-of-the-fellowship.setup.v1`) separately. The rulebook PDF is included in this repository at `manuals/lotr-fate-of-the-fellowship_core.pdf`, with redistribution authorized; it has no text layer, so the guide was written from the rendered pages. Build your match offers the player count (1–5, including the solo game), the five difficulty levels, the objectives (random or one of the rulebook's suggested scenarios for that level), and, with the "Your First Game" objectives, the suggested first-game characters. The setup counts (events, starting hands, Skies Darken stacks and their sizes) follow these choices. Character abilities, objective requirements, event effects and the two special shadow cards stay on the cards. The code is `lotr-fate-of-the-fellowship.js`; the cover and the individual icon crops are in `assets/covers/` and `assets/lotr-fate-of-the-fellowship/`.

The Witcher: Path of Destiny also uses that skill. Its guide is at `#/witcher-path-of-destiny` and stores its match (`field-manual.witcher-path-of-destiny.v1`) and checklist (`field-manual.witcher-path-of-destiny.setup.v1`) separately. It covers all seven books: the core rulebook, Stretch Goals, A Grain of Truth, Wild Hunt, Legendary Monsters, Naglfar and Geralt on Roach. They are included in this repository as `manuals/witcher-path-of-destiny_*.pdf`, with redistribution authorized (the supplied files were renamed to fix the "Wittcher" misspelling and the "a-gain-of-truth", "nagflar" and "wilshunt" typos). Build your match starts with the boxes you own, then the mode: competitive Tale (eight Tales, three of them in the core box), Wild Hunt (one player against the others, or solo against the Wild Hunt Automa), or Legendary Monsters (co-operative, against one of five monsters). Then come the player count, the Tale, the first-play simplifications, the solo difficulty, and the Tale modules (Companions, Villains, the Voice, Geralt on Roach). Naglfar is offered only in Wild Hunt, and Legendary Monsters has its own Companion and Runestone difficulty choices. Modes and modules the books declare incompatible are hidden, and any automatic adjustment is announced. Every printed page number matches its PDF page. Card texts, Tale cards and Monster cards stay on the components. The code is `witcher-path-of-destiny.js`; the cover and the icon crops are in `assets/covers/` and `assets/witcher-path-of-destiny/`.

Cangaceiros also uses that skill. Its guide is at `#/cangaceiros` and stores its match (`field-manual.cangaceiros.v1`) separately. The rulebook PDF is included in this repository at `manuals/cangaceiros_core.pdf`, with redistribution authorized. This core box has no expansions or maps, so Build your match offers a single choice: the exact player count (2–5), which decides the board/Fazenda-tile side, the Volantes deck front, and every player-count-scaled card draw (Serra, Caatinga, and Life Goal cards). The 20 Chief cards, 14 Events, and the Starting/Serra/Caatinga Action card texts are reproduced in full in the matching sections; printed Guns/Protection/Facão values and wound-space counts stay on each physical Chief card. Every printed page number matches its PDF page. The code is `cangaceiros.js`; the cover and the icon crops are in `assets/covers/` and `assets/cangaceiros/`.

Coup also uses that skill. Its guide is at `#/coup` and stores its match (`field-manual.coup.v1`, language, player count, the two-player variant, and checklist progress) separately. The 8-page core booklet is included at `manuals/coup_core.pdf`, with redistribution authorized, and it is registered in `server.py`. Every printed page number matches its PDF page. Build your match asks for the player count from 2 to 6 — the booklet never prints that range, and the guide offers it because the box has 6 summary cards and a two-player section — and, only at 2 players, the printed variant that replaces the deal. At 2 players the starting player always receives 1 coin, whether or not the variant is on. The back page only advertises the Reformation expansion, so those roles are not in this guide. Character actions, blocks, challenges and the worked example are transcribed from the booklet. The code is `coup.js`; the cover and the five character portraits are in `assets/covers/` and `assets/coup/`.

The Lord of the Rings: Duel for Middle-earth also uses that skill. Its guide is at `#/lotr-duel-for-middle-earth` and stores its match (`field-manual.lotr-duel-for-middle-earth.v1`, language plus checklist progress) separately. Two source PDFs are included in this repository with redistribution authorized: the 8-page rulebook at `manuals/lotr-duel-for-middle-earth_core.pdf` and the 2-page Player Aid at `manuals/lotr-duel-for-middle-earth_player-aid.pdf`; both are registered in `server.py` and every printed page number matches its PDF page. The game is always exactly 2 players (Fellowship of the Ring vs. Sauron) and the core box has no expansions, variants or optional rules, so Build your match has nothing to configure and the guide opens straight into Setup. The 7 Landmark tile effects, the 6 Quest of the Ring bonus spaces and the 18 Alliance token effects are transcribed from the Player Aid with page links; individual Chapter card costs and effects, and each tile's Skill cost, stay on the components. The code is `lotr-duel-for-middle-earth.js`; the cover and the icon, diagram, track and board crops are in `assets/covers/` and `assets/lotr-duel-for-middle-earth/`.

Munchkin also uses that skill. Its guide is at `#/munchkin` and stores its match (`field-manual.munchkin.v1`, language, player count, the two optional faster-play rules from p. 6, and checklist progress) separately. The 6-page core rulesheet is included at `manuals/munchkin_core.pdf`, with redistribution authorized, and it is registered in `server.py`. Every printed page number matches its PDF page. Build your match asks for the player count from 3 to 6 and, as independent yes/no choices, Listen At The Door and shared victories. Combining sets, expansions, and other products advertised on p. 6 are not covered. Monster Bad Stuff, individual curse wording, and full Class/Race printed abilities stay on the cards; the rulesheet example and general rules are in the guide. The code is `munchkin.js`; the cover is `assets/covers/munchkin.webp`.

Pandemic also uses that skill. Its guide is at `#/pandemic` and stores its match (`field-manual.pandemic.v1`: language, which of the three expansion boxes you own, player count, difficulty and the eight challenges) separately from its setup checklist (`field-manual.pandemic.setup.v1`), which resets whenever the configuration changes. Four source PDFs of 8 pages each are included with redistribution authorized and registered in `server.py`: `manuals/pandemic_core.pdf`, `manuals/pandemic_on-the-brink.pdf`, `manuals/pandemic_in-the-lab.pdf` and `manuals/pandemic_state-of-emergency.pdf`; every printed page number matches its PDF page. Build your match asks which boxes are on the table (On the Brink, In the Lab, State of Emergency), the player count (2–4, or 2–5 with On the Brink), the difficulty (Introductory, Standard, Heroic, and Legendary with On the Brink) and, as independent yes/no choices, the Virulent Strain, Mutation, Bio-Terrorist, Lab, Quarantines, Hinterlands, Emergency Events and Superbug challenges; the manuals' compatibility matrix is enforced, so incompatible pairs (such as Superbug with Mutation) are blocked with the page that says so, and Superbug automatically brings Quarantines with it. The guide then filters the inventory, the setup checklist, the roles, the Event cards and the challenge rules down to that exact match. The In the Lab Solo and Team games are out of scope. City connections, city populations, the wording of individual Virulent Strain Epidemic, Sequence and Emergency Event cards, and anything the manuals print only as artwork stay on the components and are flagged as such instead of being invented. The code is `pandemic.js`; the cover is `assets/covers/pandemic.webp` and the icon crops are in `assets/pandemic/`.
